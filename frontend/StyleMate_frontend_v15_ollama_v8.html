STYLEMATE DATASET MVP · Product names, IDs, categories, gender, colour, usage, season and images are fetched from the public Hugging Face Transformersx/fashion-product-images-small dataset. The dataset contains 22,036 records; this prototype loads a bounded sample (normally 1,000 records) to keep the app responsive and shows the exact category counts for the records it loaded. The source does not provide retail prices, sizes, inventory, reviews, return history or fit measurements. Prices shown are clearly labelled illustrative demo prices; no discount, brand, fit prediction or return-risk claim is inferred from the dataset. Image links are fetched fresh from Hugging Face when the app opens. This is a front-end prototype: checkout, stock, retailer orders and real wardrobe history are not connected. Demo sign-in is test / test. Search uses the configured local Ollama model when available and falls back to keyword search.
